Privacy policy

This page explains the optional analytics used by the hosted website and the local-first data handling of the Windows prototype.

This page needs owner and legal review before production publication.

Hosted website analytics

The hosted website uses Vercel Web Analytics only after you choose Accept. Until then, the analytics script is not loaded. Choosing Reject keeps it disabled. You can reopen this choice with Privacy settings in the footer.

Vercel says Web Analytics does not use third-party cookies. It may process page views, page URLs, referrers, approximate location, and browser or device details to provide site statistics. Read{" "} Vercel's Web Analytics privacy information .

Your choice is saved in this browser's local storage, not in a cookie. If local storage is unavailable or the choice cannot be saved, analytics remains disabled. No analytics choice is sent to the desktop app.

Windows desktop prototype

The desktop app does not collect analytics, telemetry, crash reports, or usage data. Settings are stored locally. The stream key is stored in the operating system keychain, and scrubbed crash logs stay on the device unless you choose to share them yourself.

The app connects to the streaming platform you choose when you stream. The v0.1 prototype cannot go live.

Other sites

Links to GitHub and Vercel lead to services with their own privacy practices. Their policies apply when you visit those sites.
